SECURITY EXPLAINED

How SecureFPS detection works

SecureFPS reviews gameplay telemetry after each completed match. It is a safety signal to help protect fair play, not an automatic ban decision.

  • Gameplay patterns: aim behavior, reaction timing, movement, accuracy, and input consistency are evaluated.
  • Risk score: the model combines those signals into a score from 0 to 100. Lower scores indicate lower risk.
  • Review timing: the result appears after enough match events are recorded and the analysis completes.
  • Privacy: account and sign-in signals are used only as supporting security context.
